## Hermes Bot

- **Name:** Hermes Bot
- **Tagline:** Your personal AI agent, hands-free.
- **Description:** Talk to your own Hermes agent without reaching for your
  phone. Ask anything by voice and the answer appears in your view and is
  read aloud, then Hermes Bot listens again so the conversation flows
  naturally. Short, glanceable replies; swipe to page through longer ones.
  Pick up past conversations, switch between Fast and Smart modes, and get
  the weather or headlines in a tap. Connects privately to your own Mac over
  Tailscale. Your key never leaves your devices.

  **Requires:** a computer running [Hermes Agent](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/) with its API server on, reachable over Tailscale. Free and open source; setup guide: https://github.com/flaibot/rokid-glasses-bots
- **Try saying:** "Hi Rokid, open Hermes Bot" · "What's on my calendar
  today?" · "Smart mode" · "New conversation"

## Claude Bot

- **Name:** Claude Bot
- **Tagline:** Your coding sessions, at a glance.
- **Description:** Keep your Claude Code work moving while you're away from
  the desk. Browse every project and session on your Mac, read Claude's
  latest replies page by page, and answer by voice. Run slash commands like
  context, usage, compact or code review from a tidy menu, use one-tap quick
  replies such as "Yes, go ahead", stop a reply, or start a new session in
  any project. Every message is shown for you to confirm before it's sent.
  Connects privately to your own Mac over Tailscale.

  **Requires:** a Mac with Claude Code and the free Claude Bot bridge (a small companion server) running, reachable over Tailscale. Open source; setup guide: https://github.com/flaibot/rokid-glasses-bots
- **Try saying:** "Hi Rokid, open Claude Bot" · then tap twice to reply.

## WhatsApp Bot

- **Name:** WhatsApp Bot
- **Tagline:** Read and reply to chats, eyes up.
- **Description:** Stay on top of your WhatsApp chats without taking out
  your phone. See your latest conversations with unread counts, read
  messages page by page, and reply by voice. Say "send a WhatsApp to Mom
  saying I'm on my way" and WhatsApp Bot finds the contact, even when the
  name is misheard, and shows the message for a one-tap confirm. Nothing is
  ever sent without your tap. Runs through your own Mac over Tailscale, and
  your messages stay on your devices.

  **Requires:** a Mac running the free WhatsApp Bot bridge (a small companion server that links to your WhatsApp as a linked device), reachable over Tailscale. Open source; setup guide: https://github.com/flaibot/rokid-glasses-bots
- **Try saying:** "Hi Rokid, open WhatsApp Bot" · "Send a WhatsApp to Alex
  saying running ten minutes late" · "Read my WhatsApp messages"
